01 · The trillion-dollar workforce problem
American businesses face a two-trillion-dollar productivity problem. That is Gallup’s estimate of the annual U.S. cost of employee disengagement. People are on the payroll, but their energy, commitment, and potential are being lost.

Turnover creates another enormous business burden. Gallup’s 2019 estimate put the annual U.S. cost of voluntary turnover at one trillion dollars.

Inside a company, those losses become very concrete. A bad hire consumes recruiting time, salary, onboarding, and management attention while the team waits for results. An unfilled role delays work. A disengaged employee contributes less than they could. When a valuable person leaves, experience and relationships leave with them, and the hiring cycle starts again.

Gallup estimates that replacing a leader or manager costs around twice their annual salary. The business pays to find talent, develop it, and then replace it.

The problem is clear: companies are losing money through poor fit, weak engagement, stalled development, and avoidable turnover. They need a practical way to help people succeed throughout the working day.

That is the opportunity XQ Workforce AI is built to address.
