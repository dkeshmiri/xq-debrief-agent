A plan that is easy to practice

Making progress visible

Here is how you can keep track of what is changing. At the start, and again at agreed check-ins, each of you answers a few short questions on your own. ‘I feel heard.’ ‘I feel respected.’ ‘Our next step is clear.’ ‘We return calmly after a pause.’ Zero means never, five means about half the time, and ten means consistently. Keep both answers visible. You can also calculate a shared average, with equal weight for each of you. Then add a real example from the week, because the story helps explain the score. For example, if one person’s feeling-heard score starts at four and the other’s at six, their shared average is five. If both later choose eight, the average is eight. Your own starting point comes from the answers each of you gives. Alongside those scores, you can count completed check-ins, shared commitments, and occasions when you returned to a conversation after a pause. Choose one question to pay attention to first and record each person’s starting response separately.
